About

I am a PhD student at Korea University of Technology and Education (KoreaTech) working in computer vision and deep learning under the supervision of Prof. Muhammad Tariq Mahmood . My research focuses on depth estimation including depth from focus, stereo matching and monocular depth estimation with an emphasis on geometric principles and robust loss functions for reliable predictions. I am also interested in classical computer vision and traditional image processing methods.
